About CIN

The Central Involvement Network is a prototype designed to help CWU students discover events, organizations, and ways to get involved from one central place.

All events and organizations shown in this prototype are fictional demonstration content.

Help / FAQ

How do I find events?

Use search, filters, or the calendar view.

How do I find organizations?

Browse the organization directory and open a group for more information.

Can I submit an event?

Yes. The prototype simulates event submission and temporarily adds the event to the Events list and calendar for testing.

Are these real CWU events and clubs?

No. The content is fictional and exists only for demonstration and usability testing.
